# FAQs

## Do you offer engraving?

Yes, we offer laser engraving. We can engrave initials, symbols, shapes or longer text in various fonts (if it's in the Microsoft Office package then it is doable).

Contact us at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com). It will add 1-2 business days to the waiting time.

## Will I have to pay any duties or taxes?

Orders are shipped from the European Union. Import duties and taxes may apply upon arrival and are the responsibility of the customer.

## How long does shipping take, and is it free?

We use DHL, FedEx, or UPS courier for all deliveries.

We deliver to selected international destinations (Norway, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong SAR, Taiwan, Malaysia, Israel) Free delivery in 1-6 business days

## How can I track my order?

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an email containing the tracking number of your package.

## How do I return or exchange an item?

You may return your purchase within 30 days of the delivery date, no questions asked.

Enjoy free returns! For further details, please refer to our Shipping and Return policies (/pages/shipping-and-returns).

## My bag is faulty, what do I do?

In the event of a defect, first make contact at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) and we will take care of all the shipping procedures ourselves.

After receiving our goods back, we will refund you within 2 business days or send a replacement.

## Do your products have a warranty?

At Time Resistance, we are dedicated to crafting leather goods that stand the test of time. Our products are made with the highest quality materials and traditional craftsmanship, designed to accompany you for a lifetime. Because we believe in the durability and enduring appeal of our products, we are proud to offer a lifetime warranty (/pages/warranty) on our leather bags and accessories.

Each product may vary slightly in color or texture, due to the particularity of the tanning process and the natural products used. We do not consider it a defect, but rather a token of uniqueness. To ensure the authenticity of our products, we provide a Certificate of Guarantee. This guarantees that our leather has been tanned exclusively with natural tannins of vegetable origin and does not contain substances prohibited by law. Tannin extracts are the fundamental ingredients of the slow tanning process and give the best quality. Each item is carefully crafted and undergoes accurate quality control. Small veins, wrinkles and imperceptible scratches that eventually present on the leather prove its naturalness, rendering each skin one of a kind.

## Do I need to set up an account to place an order?

No, you can shop at Time Resistance without creating an account.

You can pay with credit/debit card, PayPal, PayPal Express Checkout, or Klarna.

We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

We use SSL certificates to protect our check-out pages.

Additional payment methods may be available in the future.

## What if I need to change or cancel my order?

It is possible to cancel an order up until the time it is dispatched.

If the status of the order is "Shipped", you have to create a return.

## Is it leather?

Time Resistance bags and accessories are made from premium quality full-grain hand-dyed vegetable- tanned Italian cowhide, known as Vachetta leather. This exquisite leather is tanned exclusively with natural tannins of vegetable origin, ensuring that it is free from toxic substances. The tannin extracts are fundamental to the slow tanning process, which preserves the leather's natural look and results in a one-of-a-kind pattern for each piece.

## Do you have seasonal sales?

No, our bags are timeless, so we don't have clearances. We use high-quality materials, and our products are made in Italy. We do not set high prices only to offer big discounts later. Therefore, significant discounts are just not possible for us. On the flip side, you will never have the unpleasant surprise of finding out that something you just bought has been discounted by 50%.

## How should I care for my bag?

We recommend adding a can of Time Resistance leather balsam to your order. Give the bag a good rub once every one or two months, depending on the frequency of use. It will moisturize the leather, keep it supple, add a healthy shine and waterproof it.
