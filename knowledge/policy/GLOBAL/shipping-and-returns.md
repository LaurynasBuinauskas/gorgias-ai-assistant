# Shipping and Returns

(https://global.timeresistance.com/pages/shipping-and-returns)

## Delivery Terms

### Delivery Time

We deliver to selected international destinations (Norway, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong SAR, Taiwan, Malaysia, Israel) Free delivery in 1-6 business days

### Duties and Taxes

Orders are shipped from the European Union. Import duties and taxes may apply upon arrival and are the responsibility of the customer.

### Handling and Dispatch

- Orders placed before 2:00 AM ET on business days are processed and dispatched the same day.

- Orders placed after 2:00 AM ET, on weekends, or public holidays will be processed on the next   business day.

- A confirmation email with tracking information will be sent once your order has been dispatched.

### Shipping Partners

- UPS

- DHL

- FedEx

Delivery Tracking: All orders come with a tracking number. A tracking link will be emailed once your order has been dispatched.

Delivery Issues: If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated timeframe, contact our customer service team at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) for assistance.

Lost or Damaged Packages: Contact us immediately at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) if your package is lost or damaged in transit. We will resolve the issue and ensure you receive your order or a replacement.

Incorrect Address: Ensure the shipping address provided is correct and complete. We are not responsible for orders lost or delayed due to incorrect or incomplete addresses. Contact us immediately if you realize your address is incorrect.

Stolen Packages After Delivery: If your order is confirmed as delivered but goes missing or is stolen afterward, we're unfortunately unable to take responsibility, and a refund or replacement cannot be issued.

Contact Us: For questions or concerns about our delivery policy:

- Email: care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com)

- Website: global.timeresistance.com (/)

## Returns

At Time Resistance, we strive to ensure every customer is fully satisfied with their purchase. If any issue arises with your order, please contact our customer service office by email at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com)or fill in the "Returns form" here (https://global.timeresistance.com/pages/returns-form).

Purchasing online can be challenging since you can't feel, hold, or try on the item. If your order arrives and the product is too big, small, heavy, or uncomfortable, please initiate a return here (https://global.timeresistance.com/pages/returns-form)using your email and order number.

Once you've filled out the "Returns form (https://global.timeresistance.com/pages/returns-form)" we will contact you and provide you with a return address.

Customers from the US, UK, DE, FR and IT are provided with a local return address

Customers from the rest of the world are provided with a Lithuanian return address

### Return Policy

- You can return your purchase within 30 days from the delivery date.

- The item must be in its original box, unused, and include all parts and accessories.

- Postage is at buyer's cost. Please keep the receipt as proof of posting as we cannot be held   responsible for items lost in the mail.

- Personalized items cannot be returned for a refund.

Regarding orders that include a gift with purchase (GWP): If you decide to return an item from such an order, the GWP must also be returned in its original, unused condition. If the gift is not returned, its value may be deducted from the refund amount.

### Returning a Damaged or Defective Product

- In the unlikely event that the bag you receive is defective or damaged in transit, please contact us   by email within 30 days. A fault is a defect in appearance or function and should be apparent   immediately or after initial use.

- Contact us by e-mail (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) first regarding the defect, and we will handle   all shipping procedures. After receiving the returned goods, we will refund you or send a   replacement.

After we receive the returned item, we will reimburse your payment within 2 working days.
