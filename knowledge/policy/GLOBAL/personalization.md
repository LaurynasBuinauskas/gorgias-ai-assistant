# Personalization

(https://global.timeresistance.com/pages/personalization)

At Time Resistance, we believe that your leather accessory should be as unique as you are. Our personalization services allow you to add a touch of individuality to your favorite pieces, making them truly yours. Whether you choose laser engraving or a classic monogram, our skilled artisans will create a bespoke item that reflects your personal style and story.

## Laser Engraving

Experience the art of precision with our laser engraving service. We can engrave text, symbols, and logo onto your chosen leather product, adding a distinctive mark that stands the test of time. To personalize your item with laser engraving, simply provide the following information in the Order Special Instructions section in Your Cart when placing your order.

- Message: Specify the text you wish to engrave.

- Font Style: Choose from three elegant fonts.

- To engrave symbols or a logo, please email PNG file to care@timeresistance.com   (mailto:care@timeresistance.com). Once we receive your order, our team will create a mockup of the engraving and send it to you for your approval.

## Laser Engraving Examples

Laser engraving reacts with the leather surface, producing a unique shade each time that depends on the leather's color; the engraving color is determined by the leather and cannot be freely selected.

## Monograms (Debossing)

For a timeless and refined touch, consider adding a monogram to your leather accessory. Our debossing service imprints your initials into the leather, creating a subtle yet striking effect. To personalize your item with a monogram, simply provide the following details in the Order Special Instructions section in Your Cart when placing your order.

- Initials: Specify up to 5 characters in upper case letters.

- Preferred Color: Choose from Clear, Black, Silver, or Gold. Our monograms are crafted using the Georgia Standard font, ensuring a classic and sophisticated appearance.

## Important Information

Personalization services, including logo laser engraving and monogramming, are currently free of charge when purchased with a Time Resistance bag. This may change in the future if personalization fees are introduced. Personalized items are crafted especially for you and cannot be returned. Please ensure that all personalization details are accurate before submitting your order. Personalization takes additional 1-2 business days. If we do not receive a response to our email regarding personalization suggestions within two weeks, we will proceed with standard product customization.

## Availability

Please note that the availability of personalization options depends on the specific product and its characteristics. Some products are suitable for laser engraving, while others are best suited for monograms. While adding a product to your cart, the available personalization option for that product will be displayed.
