# Shipping and Returns

(https://eu.timeresistance.com/pages/shipping-and-returns)

## Delivery Terms

### Delivery Time

- The European Union: 1-5 business days free delivery

### Duties and Taxes

- The European Union: We guarantee no additional taxes, duties, or customs fees for customers from the   European Union.

### Handling and Dispatch

- Orders placed before 7am UTC on business days are processed and dispatched the same day.

- Orders placed after 7am UTC, on weekends, or public holidays will be processed on the next business   day.

- A confirmation email with tracking information will be sent once your order has been dispatched.

### Shipping Partners

- UPS

- DHL

- FedEx

Delivery Tracking: All orders come with a tracking number. A tracking link will be emailed once your order has been dispatched.

Delivery Issues: If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated timeframe, contact our customer service team at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) for assistance.

Lost or Damaged Packages: Contact us immediately at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) if your package is lost or damaged in transit. We will resolve the issue and ensure you receive your order or a replacement.

Incorrect Address: Ensure the shipping address provided is correct and complete. We are not responsible for orders lost or delayed due to incorrect or incomplete addresses. Contact us immediately if you realize your address is incorrect.

Stolen Packages After Delivery: If your order is confirmed as delivered but goes missing or is stolen afterward, we're unfortunately unable to take responsibility, and a refund or replacement cannot be issued.

Contact Us: For questions or concerns about our delivery policy:

- Email: care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com)

- Website: eu.timeresistance.com (/)

## Returns

At Time Resistance, we strive to ensure every customer is fully satisfied with their purchase. If any issue arises with your order, please let us know, and we will resolve it as soon as possible.

Purchasing online can be challenging since you can't feel, hold, or try on the item. If your order arrives and the product is too big, too small, too heavy, or uncomfortable, you can initiate a return here (https://timeresistance.loopreturns.com/#/) using your email and order number. Alternatively, you can contact our customer service team at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com)

### Return Policy

- You can return your purchase within 30 days from the delivery date.

- The item must be in its original box, unused, and include all parts and accessories.

- Return postage is at the buyer's cost. Please keep the receipt as proof of posting, as we cannot be   held responsible for items lost in the mail.

- Personalized items cannot be returned for a refund.

Regarding orders that include a gift with purchase (GWP): If you decide to return an item from such an order, the GWP must also be returned in its original, unused condition. If the gift is not returned, its value may be deducted from the refund amount.

### Local Returns

- Once you initiate a return (https://eutimeresistance.loopreturns.com/#/), we will provide the   nearest return address based on your location.

- Customers from the European Union will be given a return address in Italy, Germany, France,   Lithuania, or Poland, based on the most convenient location for them.

- Customers from the rest of the world will be provided with a return address in Lithuania or the US.

### Returning a Damaged or Defective Product

- In the unlikely event that the bag you receive is defective or damaged in transit, please contact us   (/pages/contact-us) within 30 days. A fault is a defect in appearance or function and should be   apparent immediately or after initial use.

- Contact us (/pages/contact-us) first regarding the defect, and we will handle all shipping   procedures. After receiving the returned goods, we will refund you or send a replacement.

Once the returned item arrives at our main headquarters, our team manually verifies the return. Please allow up to one week for the refund to be processed and credited to your original payment method.

To speed up the refund process, please contact us at care@timeresistance.com (mailto:care@timeresistance.com) with your return tracking number as soon as the package has been shipped.
