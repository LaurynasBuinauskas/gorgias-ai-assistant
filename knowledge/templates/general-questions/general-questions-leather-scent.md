# General Questions: LEATHER SCENT

Thank you for reaching out to us.

I want to assure you that the scent you’re experiencing will naturally diminish over time. It's important
to note that our bags are made from vegetable-tanned leather, a process that uses natural tannins
instead of toxic chemicals like chromium, which is commonly used in the tanning of leather (found in
around 90% of leather worldwide). This natural method is much more expensive, and as a result,
only a small percentage of leather is tanned this way. This unique process may be the reason why
the scent is different from what you may be used to.

Please let me know if this explanation helps or if you have any further concerns. I'm happy to assist
you further!
