# General Questions: LINING

Thank you for your inquiry!

For most of our products, we use a cotton blend for the lining, consisting of approximately 60-70%
cotton. This blend makes the material easier to clean and maintain. Additionally, we apply a special
coating to enhance its durability and wear resistance.
