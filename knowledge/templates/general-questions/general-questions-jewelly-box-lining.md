# General Questions: JEWELLY BOX LINING

Thank you for your question!

The interior lining of our jewelry boxes is made of soft velvet, supported by a durable wooden frame
underneath. This combination was carefully selected to provide a gentle surface for your items,
ensure long-lasting durability, and make cleaning easy while protecting against friction and wear over
time.

If you have any other questions or need further information, feel free to reach out — we're always
happy to help!
