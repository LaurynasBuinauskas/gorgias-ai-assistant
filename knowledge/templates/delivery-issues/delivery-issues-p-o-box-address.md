# Delivery issues: P.O. BOX ADDRESS

Thank you for your order!

We regret to inform you that FedEx only accepts shipments to valid street addresses and cannot deliver
to P.O. Boxes, Army Post Office (APO), or Fleet Post Office (FPO) addresses. To ensure the smooth and
timely delivery of your package, could you kindly provide us with an alternative shipping address?

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and truly appreciate your prompt attention to this
matter.

Once we receive the updated address details, we will dispatch your order immediately.

I look forward to your reply!
