# Personalization: LOGO REMOVAL

Thank you for reaching out to us.

We would like to inform you that we do not offer the option to remove or change the Time Resistance logo
from our products. Our logo is an essential part of our brand identity and is thoughtfully placed on every
item to complement the design without being intrusive.

While the logo remains fixed, we do offer additional personalization options, such as monogramming or
engraving, which can be added to your product as per your preference.

If you have any questions or would like to explore personalization options, please feel free to let us know.
We’re happy to assist!
